# Security Policy

First of all: thank you. If you're reading this, you probably found something,
and you're choosing to tell us instead of the whole internet. We appreciate that a lot.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **don't open a public issue**. Instead:

- Email: security@calicata.dev
- Or use GitHub's **"Report a vulnerability"** button (private advisory) in the affected repo.

Helpful things to include:
- What's affected (repo, version, endpoint)
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The impact as you see it
- How you'd like to be credited (name, handle, or anonymous)

## What you can expect from us

- We'll confirm we received your report.
- We'll keep you updated while we investigate and fix it.
- We'll credit you publicly once it's resolved, unless you'd rather stay a mystery.
- We won't take legal action against good-faith research that follows this policy.

## Please don't

- Access, modify or delete data that isn't yours
- Run denial-of-service tests or spam
- Social-engineer our team (we're two people; we'd notice)

Security is a team sport. Thanks for playing on our side. 🦀🔐
